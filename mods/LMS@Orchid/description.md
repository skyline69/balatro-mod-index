# Orchid
Orchid aims to elevate the vanilla experience, giving you new strong options for scoring. Scaling retriggers, Chips multipliers, a new hand type, new decks, and my own twist on making you think about chances a bit differently within the game.

## New content includes:
- 15 new Jokers
- 5 new Decks
- 1 new hand type (6-7)
- 1 new Planet Card (for said hand type)
- 1 new Tarot Card (Weakness)

## Compatibilities
- Multiplayer compatible/friendly
- JokerDisplay compatible
- Should work fine with anything else you might decide to go with
