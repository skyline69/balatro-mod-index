# Mahrlatro
Mahrlatro is another installment of the yearly "Mahr game" tradition adopted by our community, this time materialized into a Balatro mod that brings to life one of our universe's most beloved figures: **TheMahr**.

This mod adds a myriad of new content, such as jokers (_Jokahrs_), seals, consumables, decks and skins.

## Additions
* 138 Jokers (includes completely new jokers and vanilla reskins)
* 3 new Consumables and vanilla Consumable reskins (Tahrot, Plahrnet and Spectrahrl Cards)
* 1 new Seal: TheMahr Seal
* 2 new Decks:
  * TheMahr Deck
  * The Mahrket Deck
* 3 new Boss Blinds:
  * The Mahrchine
  * The Mahr
  * The Mahrnster
* 1 new card Enhancement
* 1 new card skin
* New background music
* New title screen background shader

## Miscellaneous
The mod also includes a toggle to only play with Mahrlatro jokers.
