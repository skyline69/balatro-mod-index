Joker evolutions is a Balatro mod that adds a new evolution system for Jokers. Every round the progress bar goes up, it gains evolution progress, and once it is ready, you can spend money to evolve it into a stronger and completely new version.

The mod includes 150 custom Joker evolutions, a new Evolutionary Deck, and the Catalyst Spectral Card, which can instantly evolve a random Joker for free. Maybe you can 1v1 someone with this mod and make a video out of it (link discord in description if you do)
