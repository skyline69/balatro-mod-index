In the shop it allows you to gamble your Jokers in a game of Baccarat to Enchant them with a random Edition or risk destroying them! 

You can either do one Joker at a time or multiple at once. 

If you do multiple Jokers at once, either all will be upgraded to a random Edition each or all will be destroyed. 1 Hand of Baccarat, 1 outcome.

A tie will result in nothing being gained or lost. Does not let the player bet on Tie because it wouldn't be a good bet.

The probability of winning between Player and Banker is exactly 50/50. Normally in Baccarat the House has the edge, this is fixed by if that edge happens, internally it forces a reroll.

Edition Probabilities 
40% Foil
30% Holographic 
20% Polychrome
10% Negative 

Works well with different resolutions on PC. 
Tested on Lovely Mobile Maker with an Android Fold phone and works perfect on Android phones as well. 
Added support for iOS Mobile Maker too, but don't have a way to test it. 

Installation instructions
Install the latest version of Steammodded. Extract the Baccarat Enchant folder into the Mods folder.
