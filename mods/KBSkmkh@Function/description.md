Function 0.0.1 Beta

The first beta release of Function, a Balatro mod based around mathematical concepts and mechanics.

Features

- Limit
- Derivative
- Integral
- L'Hôpital
- Series Product
- Tetration
- Fundamental Theorem
- Power Self
- Convergence
- And more

Requirements

- Balatro
- Steamodded

Suggested Mods

- Amulet
- Frost_Utils

These are suggested for compatibility/features and are not required dependencies.
