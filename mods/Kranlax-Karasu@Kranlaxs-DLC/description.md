(For a better experiencie, install the Warp Zone mod too, alongside this mod)

This mod was meant to be an dlc for the vanilla game, keeping some balance and unique mechanicsto enhance a longer playtime in the game :3

This mod adds:
- 165 brand new jokers + some extras hidden
- 7 difficulties higher than Gold stake
- 10 brand new Challenges
- 10 new decks
- 8 vouchers
- 52 new consumables (in 3 new kind of categories)
- 6 seals
- 8 booster packs
- 12 tags
- 2 stickers
- 5 editions
- and some micelaneous extras (also some kinky [not nsfw] sprites, but you must active a hidden option)
   Now the mod allows you to sort the jokers from colletion by 4 kinds of sortings
   there is an option to enable an Unbalanced mode where some jokers gains previus values before some balance changes

This mod is compatible with any other mod that you have installed

the mod is translated into
- English
- Español
- Português

what's new? 

Ver. 1.6.0

NEW CONTENT
- Added 9 new Jokers:
  + Scarf: Gains +2 Mult for each played hand that contains a Flush.
  + Belt: X0.5 Mult for each hand size below 8.
  + Broken VHS: When a hand is played, 1 in 6 chance to repeat the played cards.
  + Night Lamp: 1 in 4 chance to give +2 hand size in this round.
  + Dark Hallway: ^2 Mult, -4 hand size.
  + Broken Modem: 1 in 5 chance to retrigger each card.
  + Alien Joker: -1 selection limit, X2 Mult.
  + Ophiuchus: Gives 4$ for each scored card with a specific enhancement. (Randomly chooses an enhancement present in your deck; 15% chance to choose one not in your deck; if all enhancements are in the deck, chooses completely at random).
  + Berserker Joker: When a Boss Blind is defeated, gives X3 Mult for 45 seconds (real time).
- Difficulty Changes: "Platinum Stake" renamed to "Cosmic Stake". Added new "Mustard Stake".

HOTFIXES (BUG FIXES)
- Black Market Tokens: Fixed an issue where you couldn't buy Jokers if you didn't have their equivalent value in dollars. Purchases are now strictly independent and use tokens even if you have less than 10$ or negative money.
- Shop Size Jokers: Fixed an incompatibility between shop-increasing Jokers, V.I.P Subscription, and More Stock where rerolling the shop caused only 2 boosters to appear instead of the correct amount.
- Zenith Beanstalk: Fixed a bug where it wouldn't create God Seeds.
- Anubis: Fixed an issue where score balancing wasn't working correctly.
- Witch: Fixed an issue where it would try to debuff a card even if you didn't possess it.
- Prosopagnosia: Fixed a bug where it sometimes wouldn't debuff face cards, and the restriction would disappear if you exited and re-entered the run.
- The High Priestess?: Now correctly decreases your money (for the 10th time).
- Mod Editions: Cursed, Underwater, and Monochrome editions now properly apply their effects when evaluated on a Joker.

BUFFS
- Charismatic Bard: Removed the penalty that decreased hand size.
- Cola Soda: Decay slowed down to last longer (from -0.0025 to -0.001).
- Influencer: Mult increased from +75 to +100.
- Potato Bag (formerly Potato Chips): Improved name, and requirement reduced from 2750 to 1000 chips.
- Life of the Party: XMult increased from X0.2 to X0.25.

NERFS
- Scorpio: Price increased from 3 Tokens to 5 Tokens.
- Pink Seal: eMult lowered from ^1.75 to ^1.1.
- Bottled Water: Penalty decrement increased from -0.01 to -0.02.
- Bunny: Mult scaling reduced from X0.5 to X0.1.
- Paratrooper: Implemented a maximum decrease limit of +20. (Also made compatible with Eternal).
- Sagittarius: Mult reduced from +5 to +4.
- Taco: Removed the ability to increase its own XMult.
- Veteran Joker: Chips reduced from +20 to +15.
- Simon Says: XMult lowered from X0.25 to X0.2.
- Tagger: +Mult lowered from +10 to +5.
- Medium: Chips lowered from +5 to +1.
- Soap: Rarity lowered from Zenith to Rare.

REWORKS & ADJUSTMENTS
- Black Quartz (Obsidian): Added 2 new options: "+10 Tokens" and "Set Ante to 1".
- Yellow Quartz (Topaz): Readjusted probabilities. On Ante 7 or lower: 55% Common, 30% Uncommon, 15% Rare. Above Ante 7: 55% Rare, 30% Zenith, 15% Legendary.
- Lapis Lazuli & Graphite/Cinnabar: The bonus is no longer just added to the score; it now permanently adds the bonus to the first scored card. The consumable is now destroyed when its charges reach 0.
- Wildcard (UNO Card): Mechanical rework, now t gives the Wild enhance to all selected cards and gives them a random edition aswell.
- Black Market Evil %: The Evil percentage now decreases to 5% only when you press the Black Market button, rather than decreasing automatically when it appears.
- Cigar: Blind size increase now happens regardless of whether the cigar depletes naturally, is sold, or is destroyed. It also appears more frequently in the shop after being destroyed/sold.
- Death Synergy: Granny Joker, Devious Sheep, Russian Roulette, and Speedrunner now have proper synergy with Life Assurance.
- Post-it: Made incompatible with Blueprint (because it acts as a Blueprint itself).
- Aquarius: Grants the edition only once per Ante, and now shakes when active.
- Holy Water: Wiki description updated; now clarifies it guarantees a Joker on the next run.
- Philosopher: Added a safety check to ensure it doesn't predict anything when there are 4 or fewer cards left in the deck.
- Salsa Dancers: Effect is now applied to the whole hand as long as it contains a Pair.
- Smuggler: Ensured its effect is properly stackable.
- Zodiac Knight: The Joker itself now contains the 13 effects internally and doesn't depend on external parameters.
- Cancer: Luck-increasing effects no longer incorrectly alter its death prevention mechanic (as it could make you unable to loose).
- Chronometer: The money reward is now granted only once per Ante.
- Coupon Strip: The Joker now depletes after you buy exactly 10 items (any item from the shop).
- Telescope: Probability parameters improved. 50% to pick the most played hand, 30% to pick any other hand with a level > 1. If there are no upgraded hands, 20% to pick a level 1 hand (otherwise it merges with the previous 30% to make it 50/50).
- The 3 Rs: Made incompatible with Blueprint.
- Greedy Leprechaun: Clarified that on the first discard, it will draw 4 cards regardless of how many were discarded. Made compatible with Blueprint.
- Tsunami: Wiki description corrected. It now clearly states it applies X4 Chips when hand size is 10 or more.

VISUALS & AUDIO
- Cookies: Now plays an oven bell sound when the cookies are ready.
- Russian Roulette: Added revolver sound effects (a trigger click on a safe fail, and a gunshot if the roulette kills you).
- Mod Editions: The 5 custom editions now have their own individual sound effects.
- Monochrome: Now properly plays the exponential Mult sound effect.
- Deranged: The sprite face changes strictly after the hand finishes scoring, not before.
