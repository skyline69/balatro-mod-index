# Monopoly Mod

**Monopoly Mod** is a Balatro mod that adds the new Joker "Monopoly Guy", bringing an economic twist to your runs.

The mod currently includes custom Joker focused on money management, it's a Joker that starts with a high payout and gradually decreases its reward after each round.

### Features

* New economical Joker.
* Economy-focused effects.
* VERY OP JOKER.

The mod is still a work in progress, so some mechanics and content may be changed, balanced, or expanded over time.
