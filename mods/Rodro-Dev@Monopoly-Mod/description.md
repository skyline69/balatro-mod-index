# Monopoly Mod

**Monopoly Mod** is a Balatro mod that adds the new custom Economical Jokers, bringing an economic twist to your runs.

The mod currently includes custom Jokers focused on money management, a Deck that give you a economy impulse.

### Adds
-- **3 Jokers**
-- **1 Blind**
-- **1 Deck**

---
### Features

* New custom economical Jokers.
* Economy-focused Deck and Blind.

The mod is still a work in progress, so some mechanics and content may be changed, balanced, or expanded over time.
