Ever wonder what Balatro would be like if your deck persisted between runs?

Balatro: Reshuffled is a total conversion that reworks the game into a roguelite. Your cards, upgrades, and progress carry forward on each deck, and the whole run structure is rebuilt around that idea. Discover the rest in-game.

Public beta (v0.3.0-beta). I've playtested this build myself, but it's still a beta, you might hit a bug or a crash. If something breaks, grab the newest log from %AppData%\Balatro\Mods\lovely\log and let me know what you were doing.


Compatibility

Play this mod by itself. Do not mix with other total-conversion or large content mods (Cryptid, Pokermon, etc.).

Optional QoL UI mods should be fine but are untested. Score-predictor mods may show wrong numbers because Reshuffled rebalances scoring.
