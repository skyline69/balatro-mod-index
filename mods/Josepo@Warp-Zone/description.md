Warp Zone is a carefully balanced mod to feel more like a natural expansion for the vanilla game. Designed with seamless integration in mind, all of these new Jokers are 100% compatible with any other mods you have installed.

This mod adds:
- 150 jokers
- 30 blind bosses
- 10 challenges
- 15 decks

The mod is fully translated into multiple languages:
- English
- Español
- Português
