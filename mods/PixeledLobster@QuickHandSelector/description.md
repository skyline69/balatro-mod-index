# Quick Hand Selector

Adds a draggable panel to the in-run HUD with buttons that select a hand or a
combination directly. One button per poker hand your hand can currently make, four
suit buttons, and side actions to invert the selection, add your lowest cards up to
the limit, or clear everything. Pressing the same hand button again cycles through the
alternative combinations for it.

Extra buttons appear only when relevant, for debuffed and face-down cards, and an
optional copy of the game's own Play and Discard buttons can sit on either side. Every
button works from the keyboard, all keys are rebindable from the game's options menu,
and right-clicking any button runs its opposite.

You still decide what to play — the panel just saves you picking the cards out one by
one. It never reads hand levels, chips, mult or jokers, and it never reveals face-down
cards.

Lovely only — Steamodded is not required.
